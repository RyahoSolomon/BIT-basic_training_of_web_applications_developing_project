Rectangular panels with interior ornament — generation record
Method: built-in image generation. Reference: design/ui/button_64.png.
Purpose: style selection, manual downsampling and pixel refinement. No runtime integration or nine-slice extraction performed.
Actual outputs are 2-by-2 presentation sheets with a brown backdrop, despite the transparent-background request. Crop individual rectangular panels before preparing runtime assets.

ui-panels-inner-patterns-a-v1.png
Use case: ui-mockup.
Generate a NEW pixel-art UI BASE PANEL DESIGN COLLECTION for the same Chinese fantasy browser game. The supplied reference button is ONLY a reference for warm cream / brown palette and chunky pixel-art treatment; DO NOT copy its chamfered silhouette.
User's highest priority: ALL FOUR PANELS HAVE STRICTLY RECTANGULAR OUTER BOUNDARIES, while all ornamental motifs are INSIDE the panel. This is the opposite of protruding decorated corner frames.

Layout: one landscape sheet, a neat 2 x 2 grid of FOUR equally sized wide rectangular panels with generous transparent gutters and outer margin. Each panel approximately 1.65:1 aspect ratio. All assets fully visible and separated. No buttons, no separate icons, no swatches, no labels or explanatory text, no rulers. Use the available image resolution for the four complete panel designs.
Geometry: Every panel has FOUR PERFECTLY STRAIGHT OUTER SIDES and FOUR EXACT 90-DEGREE OUTER CORNERS. Complete square corners, no cut corners, no rounded outer corners, no notches, no scallops, no bumps, no protruding leaves/clouds/crest/flowers, no drop shadow outside. All four panels have the SAME simple flat rectangular outer silhouette and consistent narrow dark-brown outer stroke. The literal axis-aligned bounding rectangle is completely filled with opaque panel pixels. All outside pixels are fully transparent.
Decorations: embellishment is drawn only INSIDE the rectangular perimeter, inset from the outside border. Use internal corner brackets, inward-facing motifs, and an inner line as appropriate. Keep a thin plain outer rim intact. Rounded clouds or petals may appear INSIDE the frame, never change the outer rectangle. Four internally ornamented corners per panel; at least 70 percent of the interior remains a clean blank central content area. Most straight middle edge runs are clean and uniform for nine-slice scaling, not an unbroken giant illustration. Prefer four rotationally related ornament clusters where natural. Every motif is thick and clear enough to survive downsampling.
Pixel treatment: authentic low-resolution GAME PIXEL ART, broad hard square-pixel stair steps, limited flat indexed-looking colors, regular stroke widths. Nearest-neighbor enlarged appearance. Warm opaque cream center #F2E0AE, dark chocolate outline, ochre #B17737 and medium warm brown ornament lines, a few pale-cream accents. Center must be one UNIFORM FLAT COLOR. No gradients, glow, paper texture, noise, dithering, antialiasing, soft shading, lighting, metal reflections, 3D, blurry edges, colored halos or painted transparent checkerboard.
Composition is functional sprites, not a poster or scene. NO text, numbers, watermarks, characters or objects. The design differences must be ornamental vocabulary and complexity INSIDE the same rectangle, not changes in aspect ratio or exterior shape.

Four visibly different patterns, in reading order:
TOP LEFT — RUYI CLOUDS: medium-ornate paired rounded cloud-head curls and small step spirals curling inward from each internal corner; airy, auspicious, clear cream negative space between cloud curls. Clouds remain entirely INSIDE the rectangular rim.
TOP RIGHT — SQUARE KEY-FRET: restrained geometric Chinese meander / huiwen. Clean nested right-angle square spirals in internal corners, connected by simple thin rectangular lines. The most architectural and simple of the four.
BOTTOM LEFT — LOTUS PETALS: medium-rich fan of three to five stepped pointed lotus petals inside each corner, oriented toward the panel center; small symmetrical inset lines frame the blank content area. No petals protrude outside.
BOTTOM RIGHT — SCROLLING LEAF FILIGREE: more ornate but readable inward curling vine-scroll corner ornaments with small lance-shaped leaves; brown and cream only, no green necessary. Ornament grows inward along the inner margin and leaves the center blank.
Make all four genuinely distinct. Most important: strict rectangular outer boundaries, all art inside, transparent gutters.
